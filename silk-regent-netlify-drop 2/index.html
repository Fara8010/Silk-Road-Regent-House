Christmas in London · five days / four nights

A Regent Christmas Privé

A festive London programme featuring Spencer House, Covent Garden and the capital’s landmarks, with a proposed private Christmas dinner at Fortnum & Mason. The itinerary includes Windsor, seasonal shopping and a Paxton & Whitfield experience, with time to rest between visits.

Maximum 6 couples · 12 guests · 4 nights

From £8,500 per couple

Explore the five-day itinerary
  1. Day 1 · Arrival & welcome

    Airport meet-and-greet and transfer, followed by time to settle in. Dinner details are confirmed before departure.

  2. Day 2 · Spencer House & London landmarks

    Proposed Spencer House tour, a journey around London’s landmarks and lunch in Covent Garden. Afternoon refreshments and a rest, followed by a gastropub dinner and Trafalgar Square.

  3. Day 3 · Country heritage & festive dining

    Dorney Court tour, or Hampton Court Palace as an alternative subject to availability. Lunch and a walk in Windsor, then time to rest before a proposed private Christmas dinner at Fortnum & Mason and the lights of Piccadilly and Regent Street. The final sequence is adjusted to the selected venue and dinner reservation.

  4. Day 4 · Shopping & British food heritage

    Selfridges and Bond Street, followed by Knightsbridge and Hyde Park. A proposed Paxton & Whitfield “Royal Warrant Experience” completes the day; the format and appointment are confirmed with the supplier.

  5. Day 5 · Departure

    Airport transfer and departure.

A fixed package itinerary, not available for customisation. Venue access and timings are confirmed before booking; any necessary supplier-led substitution will be communicated in advance.

Beyond the festive season · five days / four nights

Crown & Country Privé

Discover Greenwich, the Thames, Hampton Court Palace and Richmond. A proposed visit to Neal’s Yard Dairy’s Bermondsey arches and a farewell dinner complete this five-day experience.

Maximum 6 couples · 12 guests · 4 nights

From £8,500 per couple

Explore the five-day itinerary
  1. Day 1 · Arrival & a gentle introduction

    Airport meet-and-greet and transfer, time to settle in and a scheduled welcome dinner.

  2. Day 2 · Greenwich & the Thames

    Explore Greenwich’s maritime heritage, with a proposed visit to the Old Royal Naval College. Lunch and a river journey on the Thames, with the river service confirmed before booking.

  3. Day 3 · Hampton Court & Richmond

    A proposed guided visit to Hampton Court Palace and its gardens, followed by lunch and a riverside walk in Richmond. An evening at leisure completes the scheduled programme, with your bilingual concierge escort on hand.

  4. Day 4 · Behind the Arches: Neal’s Yard Dairy

    A proposed behind-the-scenes visit to Neal’s Yard Dairy’s cheese-maturation rooms in Bermondsey’s converted railway arches, followed by a tutored cheese tasting. Access is limited and must be agreed directly with the dairy; a suitable tasting alternative will be proposed if an arches visit is unavailable. Time at leisure and a farewell dinner complete the day.

  5. Day 5 · Departure

    Airport transfer and departure.

A fixed package itinerary, not available for customisation. Venue access and timings are confirmed before booking; any necessary supplier-led substitution will be communicated in advance.
