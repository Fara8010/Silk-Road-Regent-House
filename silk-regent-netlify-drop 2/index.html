The city collection / Christmas season

London Christmas Tastes

A festive London escape shaped around British food, tea and hospitality. Discover Borough Market with Celia Brooks, the art of tea at Twinings, London from the river and the glow of the Christmas lights. Wellington places your stay in the heart of Westminster.

Five nights at Wellington, with a double room for two in the heart of Westminster.

From £6,910 per couple£3,455 per person sharing

Five couples / ten guests

Explore your programme
  1. Day 1 / Tuesday · A warm London welcome

    Your arrival is met with a personal airport welcome and a smooth transfer to Wellington. Settle into Westminster before welcome gifts and a convivial dinner begin your festive London stay.

  2. Day 2 / Wednesday · London, tasted and discovered

    Discover Borough Market with Celia Brooks, enjoying the stories and flavours of its celebrated producers. Savour traditional fish and chips at Rock & Sole Plaice in Covent Garden, London’s oldest fish-and-chip restaurant, established in 1871. London’s landmarks and a Thames cruise complete the day.

  3. Day 3 / Thursday · The art of tea, the glow of Christmas

    Explore Piccadilly and Fortnum & Mason before a two-hour Classic Tea Masterclass at Twinings. As London’s Christmas lights illuminate the streets, gather for a festive dinner in a traditional gastropub.

  4. Day 4 / Friday · Style, tradition and a farewell table

    Enjoy Selfridges and Bond Street, followed by the pleasure of festive afternoon tea. Your final evening unfolds at The English Grill at The Rubens, where an elegant farewell dinner brings the journey to a close.

  5. Day 5 / Saturday · London at your own pace

    Enjoy an unhurried final day in London, with your bilingual concierge on hand. Return to a favourite neighbourhood, browse the city’s boutiques or simply pause to savour the festive atmosphere. Personal purchases and unlisted meals are separate.

  6. Day 6 / Sunday · A graceful departure

    Enjoy a final moment in London before your arranged airport transfer. Your concierge coordinates departure details, allowing you to leave with ease.

Early or mid December, outside 24–26 December closures. The cruise is a hosted scheduled sightseeing service. Restaurant menus and included drinks are agreed before booking. Twinings accommodates ten guests. Shopping purchases are separate.

Welcome dinner, Borough tastings, a heritage lunch of traditional fish and chips at Rock & Sole Plaice (est. 1871), festive gastropub dinner, festive afternoon tea, English Grill farewell dinner, Twinings masterclass, landmarks minibus and a Thames sightseeing cruise. Airport transfers, programme transport, bilingual concierge accompaniment, visa paperwork support in Uzbekistan and a selected welcome gift.

Surrey Wine & London

London’s festive food traditions meet English wine country. Borough Market, a tutored whisky tasting and a West End evening lead to an indoor cellar tasting and lunch at Denbies, with afternoon tea and time to enjoy the capital.

Five nights at Wellington, with a double room for two and central London at your doorstep.

From £6,290 per couple£3,145 per person sharing

Five to six couples / ten to twelve guests

Explore your programme
  1. Day 1 / Monday · London welcomes you

    A personal airport welcome and transfer bring you to Wellington. Settle into the city, then gather over a welcome dinner that sets the tone for the days ahead.

  2. Day 2 / Tuesday · Borough and the riverside

    Join Celia Brooks for an official Borough Market tasting tour, meeting London through its food and the people behind it. A leisurely South Bank walk reveals the capital from the river’s edge.

  3. Day 3 / Wednesday · Craft and the West End

    Travel to Denbies Wine Estate, where the Surrey Hills frame a day devoted to English wine. An indoor cellar tasting and lunch in the Gallery Restaurant introduce the estate’s wines and setting before your return to London.

  5. Day 5 / Friday · A festive London afternoon

    Savour afternoon tea and explore London’s boutiques at a considered pace. As daylight fades, the Christmas lights lend a celebratory glow to your final full day in the capital.

  6. Day 6 / Saturday · Farewell to London

    Your concierge arranges check-out and your airport transfer, leaving time for an unhurried farewell to the city.

The wine experience, theatre performance and tasting appointments are confirmed for your chosen dates before booking.
